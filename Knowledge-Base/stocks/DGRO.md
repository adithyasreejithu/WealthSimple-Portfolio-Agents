The options_activity read is directionally bullish, but the largest put-OI strike sits almost exactly at spot rather than below it, and the absolute IV levels look unusually low -- I'd treat that dimension as informative, not decisive, at its 3% weight anyway. Where I have real conviction: the expense ratio and scale (0.08%, $41.2B AUM) are unambiguously excellent, the distribution history shows a genuine decade of uninterrupted growth, and the price trend and concentration profile are clean. Those dimensions carry most of the ETF-track weight and are what is actually driving the Add. The practical caveat the rubric can't see: this position is ~0.006% of the portfolio, far below any weight that matters -- so 'Add' here is more a statement that DGRO is a good vehicle than a signal to act with urgency. If the owner wants the Income sleeve to actually matter, sizing DGRO up materially -- not just nudging it -- is the real decision, and that is a portfolio-construction call this rubric does not score.
