## Updated Thesis

The core thesis -- that Palantir is a category-defining AI/data-platform compounder whose commercial and government franchises are scaling into GAAP profitability -- is stronger than at the 2026-07-14 review on fundamentals. Revenue is growing 56% year over year, net income has more than tripled to $1.63B, net margin sits at 43.7%, ROE is 32.6%, and the balance sheet is effectively debt-free with a 7.1 current ratio. The beat-and-raise pattern continued into today's print with three analyst upgrades and no downgrades over the last 90 days. On business quality alone, the case for owning PLTR has never been cleaner.

What has not changed -- and what keeps this a Hold rather than an Add -- is valuation. A ~60x forward P/E, ~141x trailing P/E, and a 0.58% free-cash-flow yield leave essentially no margin of safety; the stock is priced for sustained flawless execution. The one cushion is that price sits ~31% below the $182 analyst mean target, but that gap reflects analyst optimism as much as genuine undervaluation. Sentiment is genuinely mixed: ratings are buy-skewed yet trailing 90d and 365d returns are negative.

Net, the thesis is unchanged in direction and verdict: a best-in-class business to hold through the compounding, but not one to add to at this multiple. The valuation ceiling caps upside conviction, so the rating stays Hold. A meaningful multiple reset toward the 3-5% FCF-yield range would be the trigger to upgrade to Add.
