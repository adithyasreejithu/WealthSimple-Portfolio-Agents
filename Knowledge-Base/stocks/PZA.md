## Analyst View

I agree with Hold. As a royalty trust, the ~98% payout is structural rather than a distress signal -- these vehicles exist to pass through nearly all cash -- so I read the dividend-safety '2' as a design feature, not a looming cut. The genuine concern is the weak price trend and the sensitivity of distributions to same-store sales. It is doing its income job; hold for the yield while watching royalty pool sales.
