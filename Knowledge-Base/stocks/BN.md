## Analyst View

I dissent from the mechanical Sell. The solvency gate is designed for operating companies, and it misreads an asset manager/holding company where consolidated leverage and 'free cash flow' are structural accounting outcomes, not signs of distress. Parent-level net income is positive and the dividend is modestly covered. I would NOT sell on this signal; I flag this as a rubric limitation for the owner to retune (asset-manager carve-out). That said, per policy the band-derived action stands as Sell and I am not overriding it.
