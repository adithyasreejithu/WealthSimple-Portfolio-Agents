## Analyst View

I agree with the mechanical Hold. The business quality is not in question; the price is. At a forward P/E of 33 and PEG of 2.6 against ~6% revenue growth, the market is paying a premium the fundamentals do not currently earn, and shares are already at the analyst mean target. I would not add here, but the FCF engine, dividend safety and momentum make trimming unnecessary. The valuation dimension is the whole debate.
