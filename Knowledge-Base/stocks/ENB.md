## Analyst View

I dissent from the mechanical Sell on nuance but respect the flag. Midstream operators routinely pay out more than GAAP net income because heavy depreciation depresses earnings while cash flow (DCF/AFFO) comfortably covers distributions; a >100% GAAP payout is normal here, not a cut signal. However, the rubric uses the earnings payout ratio and it does fail, so the band-derived action stands as Sell and I am not overriding it. The genuine risk to watch is that negative reported FCF plus high absolute leverage leaves less cushion than the DCF narrative implies. I would verify DCF payout coverage before acting.
