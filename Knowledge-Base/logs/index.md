# Logs

Append-only history of the research wiki. Rows are added by skills via
`src/kb_pages.py`; existing rows are never edited or deleted.

- [decision-log.md](decision-log.md) — dated buy/sell/hold/trim/add/reject decisions.
- [research-log.md](research-log.md) — what was researched, when, from which sources.
- [update-log.md](update-log.md) — the research wiki's change log (page created/updated and why).

Note: `Knowledge-Base/CHANGELOG.md` is **not** part of the research wiki — it
tracks versions of the classifier YAMLs in `ref/` only.
